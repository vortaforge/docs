What "independently installable" actually means

A datapack cannot import another datapack. That single constraint shapes the whole project: instead of modules calling each other, every extension point is a function tag owned by the core, and modules add themselves to it.

The consequence is checkable, not aspirational. Each of the 34 packs is built once with exactly one pack absent, and the engine has to reach load and tick anyway. All 34 pass. If that sounds like a low bar, it is the bar that separates a modular engine from a monolith with folders.

Honest limits

  • Everything archived is archived. The packs on Datapacks are listed so an old-world question has an answer. They are not maintained and not supported.
  • 26.3 only. There is no multi-version branch. The engine targets one Minecraft release and moves when that release does.
  • The two mods are narrow on purpose. Neither has a permission system, a GUI or a database. A mod that grows a second responsibility is harder to remove than the thing it replaced.
Numbers on this site come from source. 34 artifacts and 1,529 functions from modules.json, the clamps from AfkSettings / ThrottleSettings, the pack formats from archive.json. Where a page states a number, it was read, not estimated.
